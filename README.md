## Project Overview

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market in Nairobi. She has been recording her daily sales and keeping track of her stock manually. She wants to understand how her business is performing, whether sales are growing, which products need restocking and where she should focus to improve her business. This project uses 14 days of sales data and a stock dictionary to analyse business performance using core Python without pandas or NumPy.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Sales growth:** Total sales were KES 46,450, with Week 2 sales increasing by 10.66% compared to Week 1.

2. **Weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, which was 82.50% higher than weekday average sales of KES 2,685.

3. **Stock management:** Tomatoes and sukuma were below their reorder levels and need restocking.
